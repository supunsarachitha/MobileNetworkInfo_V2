Mobile Network Info

Everything about your Android phone's network, hardware, battery and sensors, in one clean app. Free · No ads · No tracking · Source available

Screenshots

A clean, modern design with automatic dark mode.

Features

Five simple tabs covering everything about your phone.

📶

Network

  • Connection status: Wi-Fi, mobile data, Ethernet or VPN, metered and roaming state, Private DNS
  • Wi-Fi details and a scanner for nearby networks
  • Mobile network: carrier, 2G/3G/4G/5G, signal strength, RSRP/RSRQ/SINR
  • Serving cell, IP & DNS details, and a connection speed test
📱

Device

  • Model, manufacturer, Android version, security patch and build details
  • Processor, memory and storage usage
  • Display resolution, density, refresh rate, HDR
  • Hardware features like NFC, eSIM, UWB and more
🔋

Battery

  • Level, status, power source and health
  • Temperature, voltage and technology
  • Charging current, charge cycles and time until full
🧭

Sensors

  • Live compass with heading and direction
  • Device tilt, accelerometer, gyroscope and magnetometer
  • Air pressure, altitude, light level and proximity
  • A list of every sensor in the phone
🛠

Everything else

  • Overview dashboard at a glance
  • Share a device report as text
  • Tap any value to copy it
  • Light and dark themes that follow your system setting

Privacy

Mobile Network Info has no ads, no analytics, no accounts and no tracking. Everything it shows is read from your phone and stays on your phone.
