# What's new

Every update to Mobile Network Info, explained in plain language. The newest version is at the top.

## Version 5.0 · September 2026

### New
- A fresh, modern design with easy-to-read cards and automatic dark mode.
- Five simple tabs: Overview, Network, Device, Battery and Sensors.
- Overview dashboard: see your connection, battery, memory and storage at a glance.
- Wi-Fi details: network name, signal strength, speed, channel, frequency band and security type.
- Wi-Fi scanner: see the Wi-Fi networks around you, how strong they are and which channels they use.
- Mobile network details: carrier, 4G/5G network type, signal strength and the cell tower your phone is using.
- Connection test: check how quickly popular servers respond to your phone.
- See your public IP address, plus your local IP addresses, router (gateway) and DNS servers.
- Battery details: health, temperature, voltage, charging current, charge cycles and time until full.
- Memory and storage usage, processor details and display details such as HDR and refresh rate.
- A list of your phone's hardware features, like NFC, eSIM and 5 GHz Wi-Fi.
- A live compass, device tilt, air pressure, altitude, light level, and a list of every sensor in your phone.
- Share a complete device report as text, handy for tech support or when selling your phone.
- Tap any value to copy it.
- Shortcuts to Android's Internet, Wi-Fi, mobile network and data usage settings.
- This "What's new" page and a page to support the app.

### Improved
- Works with the latest Android versions, including Android 16.
- Sensors only run while you're looking at them, which saves battery.
- Location access is only requested when you choose to see Wi-Fi or cell tower details, with a clear explanation of why Android needs it.

### Fixed
- The compass needle pointed in the wrong direction.
- The phone's build date was shown incorrectly.
- A possible crash on phones older than Android 12.

### Removed
- All ads. The app is now completely ad-free.
- Permissions the app didn't need, such as making phone calls and reading body sensors.
- The serial number, which newer Android versions no longer share with apps.
